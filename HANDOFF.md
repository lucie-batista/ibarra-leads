# Ibarra Leads: Guide for Ibarra Brothers Printing

Ibarra Leads finds **new businesses that just opened within 2 miles of the shop** and helps you reach out to them. It updates itself every morning. You decide who to contact, and you send every email yourself. The tool never sends anything on its own.

The passcode, the web app link and the account logins are on the **private sheet** you were given, not in this guide. Keep that sheet somewhere safe.

---

## 1. Opening the tool

1. Open the **Ibarra Leads link** (it's on the private sheet). Bookmark it on your computer and phone.
2. Type the **passcode** and click **Open**.
3. To lock the screen when you're done, click **Lock** (top right).

It takes a few seconds to load. That's normal.

---

## 2. What you see

**The row of tabs at the top** sorts businesses into ready-made lists:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **To email** | New businesses where we found an email. The easiest ones; start here. |
| **To call** | No email, but we found a phone number. |
| **Visit or postcard** | No email or phone yet. Stop by, mail a postcard, or look them up. |
| **All new** | Every new business you haven't contacted yet. |
| **In progress** | Businesses you've contacted that haven't become customers yet. |
| **Liquor licenses** | Restaurants, bars and markets anywhere in SF that just applied for a liquor license. Each is tagged **Opening soon**, **New owner** (someone is taking over) or **Adding alcohol** (a drink-menu opportunity). |
| **Customers** | Businesses that became customers. |

Under the tabs you can **search** by name or street, pick **one kind of business** (e.g. only restaurants), and sort by **best match**, **newest** or **closest**.

**Each card** shows the business name, what kind of business it is, the neighborhood, the distance from the shop, and when it opened. The colored button on the right is the **best way to reach them**:

| Button | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Email** (green) | We found their email. The easiest option. |
| **Call** (blue) | We found a phone number. |
| **Visit** (yellow) | No email or phone, but it's close by. Stop in. |
| **Postcard** (yellow) | Mail your new-customer postcard to their mailing address (a paper postcard, not email). |
| **Look up** (gray) | We didn't find contact details yet. Search for them on Google. |

The best leads are at the top: restaurants and other good fits that are new, close, and have an email.

**Results** (top right): a summary of how outreach is going. Use it to see what's working.

**Templates** (top right): change the wording of the emails, the call script and the postcard note.
- Choose a **Part to edit** (the email body, or a kind of business), change the text, and click **Save**. The preview next to it shows how a real email will look.
- **Reset to original** puts back the starting wording.
- Keep anything in {curly brackets} exactly as it is; those get filled in automatically (business name, street, and so on).
- Changes apply to every business, except ones where you already edited and saved that specific email.

## 3. Contacting a business

Click a card to open it.

### Email (three steps on the screen)
1. **Review:** read the draft. The business name and street are filled in, and the 15% new-customer offer is included. If something doesn't fit, click **Edit wording**, change it, then **Save changes**. Your changes are saved for that business.
2. **Send it from your email:** click **Open in email**. Your email program opens with everything filled in. Look it over, then **send it yourself**.
   - If your email program doesn't open, click **Copy email**, open Zoho Mail (**Open Zoho Mail** button), start a new email and paste.
   - If a message says "Message copied", the email was too long for the link. Just paste it into the body.
3. **After sending:** click **Mark as emailed**.

### Call or visit
- **Call:** click **Call** to dial. A short script is shown if you want it. Then click **Mark as called**.
- **Visit:** click **Directions** for a map from the shop. Then click **Mark as visited**.

### Postcard
- Click **Copy address** and mail your new-customer postcard. Then click **Mark postcard sent**.

### Look up
- Click **Search on Google**. If you find their email, type it in **Add or fix email** (under **Contact details**) and click **Save email**. The Email option then appears.

### Keeping track
Use the **Status** buttons on each business:

**New → Contacted → Replied → Quoted → Customer**

- If someone replies **"no thanks"** or asks not to be contacted, click **No thanks**. They disappear from the list and **must never be emailed again**. Every email promises this.
- Write anything useful in **Notes**, e.g. "Owner Maria, wants menu quote next week". Then click **Save notes**.

Keeping the statuses up to date is how the **Results** page knows what's working.

## 4. A simple weekly routine (about 30 minutes)

1. **Monday:** open the tool. It starts on **To email**. Send 5–10 emails to the businesses at the top.
2. **Midweek:** open **To call** and **Visit or postcard** and reach out to a few near the top.
3. **Whenever someone replies:** update their status (Replied, Quoted, Customer, or No thanks).
4. **Once a month:** check **Results**.

**The offer:** **15% off the first order for new customers.** No code is needed. If someone says they're a new customer, they get the discount.

---

## 4b. Weekly: add new liquor-license applications (about 2 minutes)

The state posts new liquor-license applications every day. Once a week:
1. Go to **abc.ca.gov** → **Licensing** → **Licensing Reports** → **Daily Reports: New Applications**.
2. Download the report for each day since last time (each download is a CSV file).
3. Open **Google Drive** (shop account) → the folder **ABC reports** → drag the downloaded files in.
4. That's it. The next morning's update adds the San Francisco ones to the **Liquor licenses** tab. Files already added are skipped, so a duplicate does no harm.

## 5. The Google Sheet (optional)

Everything the tool knows is in a Google Sheet called **Ibarra Leads**, in the shop's Google Drive. You can open it to look around, but you don't need to.

- **Leads:** all businesses. Please don't delete or reorder the columns.
- **Templates:** the email wording (easier to change with the **Templates** button in the tool).
- **Config:** settings, such as how far away to look (2 miles). Only change these if you know what they do.
- **Log** and **Usage:** a record of the daily updates and lookups.

## 6. Accounts (who owns what)

All accounts belong to the shop. The logins are on the private sheet.

| Account | What it's for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Google account** (shop Gmail) | Owns the tool, the Sheet and the daily updates | Free |
| **Google Cloud** (same Google account) | Finds phone numbers and websites (Google Places) | Free: stays under Google's 1,000 free lookups a month. A card is on file. |
| **Anthropic / Claude** (sign in with the shop Gmail) | Finds contact info for some businesses with AI | Prepaid credit, no automatic charges. Capped at $3 a month by the tool. |
| **Zoho Mail** (sales@ibarrabrothersprinting.com) | Where you send emails from | Your existing account |

When the Claude credit runs out, the tool keeps working; it just finds fewer emails. To add more, sign in to console.anthropic.com with the shop Google account → Billing → Buy credits.

## 8. If something breaks

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"Wrong passcode"** | Check the private sheet. After 10 wrong tries it locks for 15 minutes. |
| **Forgot the passcode / want a new one** | Go to script.google.com (shop Google account) → **Ibarra Leads** → ⚙️ **Project Settings** → **Script Properties** → **Add script property**: name `PASSCODE_NEW`, value = the new passcode → **Save**. Then open the tool and use the new passcode. |
| **"The daily update hasn't run since…"** | Open the Sheet → **Log** tab and look at the newest rows for "FAILED". Most problems fix themselves the next morning. If it keeps happening for several days, contact your helper (section 9). |
| **No new email addresses are being found** | The Claude credit may be used up, or this month's free lookups are done (they restart on the 1st). The tool still works; use Look up, Call, Visit or Postcard. |
| **"Open in email" doesn't open anything** | Use **Copy email** and paste into Zoho Mail. |
| **To lock the screen** | Click **Lock** (top right). |
| **A business is wrong or closed** | Mark it **No thanks**, or just skip it. |
| **The page won't load** | Try again in a minute, or in another browser. |
